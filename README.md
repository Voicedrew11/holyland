Holyland is a native PC port of King's Field: The Ancient City (*King's Field IV*), built from the [kfiv-decomp](https://github.com/Voicedrew11/kfiv-decomp) decompilation of the PlayStation 2 game.

## Features

- Widescreen support (16:9)
- Internal resolution up to 8x
- Anti-aliasing (FXAA, SMAA, SMAA T2x)
- Anisotropic filtering
- 60+ fps, up to unlocked
- Adjustable field of view and draw distance
- Sharp font
- Quick save and quick load anywhere
- Automap and minimap
- Extended stats (durability, ailment timers, hit log, enemy health bars)
- Hands and hotkeys for weapons, spells and items
- Keyboard and mouse support
- Modern Twin-stick FPS controls
- Adjustable game speed
- Launcher with update notices
- Dozens of other little features

## Status

Game can be played from start to finish.

There are known issues with z-fighting. The reason we haven't addressed it is because all solutions we have came up with are extremely computationally expensive. We will figure something proper out in the future.

## Requirements

A dump of the North American PlayStation 2 release (`SLUS-20318`) in `.iso` or `.chd` format.
