The owner reported: "performance is getting choked in some areas to the point where the framerate will drop to 60
fps." A diagnosis session (no code changed, nothing committed) found a worse problem first. Read all of this before
running the game.

## 0. Safety rules (read first)

  **Do not run the game to chase the hang without the owner's OK and a time they choose.** The owner was told this
  and asked to pick a time.

  `touch ~/.cache/kf4leak/stopmem`).
- One run at a time. Use `--exe out/linux-clang/kf4` with every tool (the defaults may pick a stale build).
- Disc: `~/Desktop/Verdite-Project/kfiv-decomp/baserom/kfiv_us.iso` (`KF4_DISC`). Put dumps and data folders under
  `~/.cache/<dir>`, never `/tmp` (small tmpfs), and delete them after.

### 1c. What the aftermath looks like (don't misread it)

- An 8 s gap in `KF4_PRESENT_LOG` with the game carrying on after it. It looks like a load, but it is a reset
  (17:29:56).
- Watchdog kills reading 9-12.7 GB of card VRAM, and a second of 29 pictures, around a reset (17:28). These are not
  leaks; I first read them as a "memory blow-up".
- A permanent freeze (17:32). `kf4-pc` spins at 100% in `Device::next_frame_context` → `PerFrame::begin` →
  `QueryPool::begin` → `vkGetQueryPoolResults(..., WAIT_BIT)`, holding Granite's device lock. The results never
  come, because the reset lost the context. `kf4-pc-pump` is blocked on that lock (`destroy_descriptor_pool` from
  `~Kept` → `~Table`, pcr_canvas.cpp:2133); `kf4-dma` waits in `Inbox::put`; the game waits in
  `Packet_WaitForSend`. Full stacks: `~/.cache/kf4leak/hang_bt.txt`. Granite never sees device loss here: a second,
  smaller bug (waiting forever after a lost device).

## 2. Why the rate drops to 60 (the owner's report)

Not reproduced at a teleport spot: all nine 170 Hz spots held 169.7-169.9 pictures a second, lowest second 169,
with GPU 3.7-4.4 ms a picture (the GPU at 170 is about 75% busy, at low clocks; at 240 the same frame took 3.45 ms
because the clocks rise). Per-map stats lines: `~/.cache/kf4sweep/log_*.txt`; results:
`~/.cache/kf4sweep/results.txt`.

The mechanism that makes "60" (port/platform/gs.c, `port_gs_run_vsync`, around lines 1085-1160): when the renderer
is behind for 2 vblanks, the cut takes `interp_allow = min(allow, between) - 1`. At 170 a vblank has 2-3 pictures
between, so the steps are 170 → about 120 → 60: two cuts, 30 vblanks apart. Raising back is one picture per
`interp_rest` (30, doubling up to 120 vblanks after a fall). So a place that needs only a little more GPU than the
budget can sit at 60 for seconds. At an overloaded 240, without a hang, it degraded gently (to 209-240 a second), so
the cliff needs a spot heavier than any tested.

Not yet done: find a real spot that drops. Ideas: spell or particle effects (native 700 is 0.84 ms a picture at 009
from the fire alone), many enemies, water. Also ask the owner where it happens, since they play the real game.
Also: the F3 counter shows "GAME CUT N" while cutting, so the owner can confirm whether their drops are the cut.

## 3. GPU memory (owner: "the game should not be using tons of vram")

The game's own VRAM (fdinfo) is about 4.2 GB at 009 with the owner's settings, and the card total swings by up to
1.7 GB within a quarter second. The game's data is small; the memory is screen-sized images:

- `Canvas::Impl::copy_surfaces` (pcr_canvas.cpp around 2384) runs **each game frame** (30 a second) for the pump:
  for every colour and depth surface (5-6 at 009) it makes **new** images (`make_surface_image`, plus
  `make_ms_image` with MSAA) and copies into them. At 3840x1792, colour high (16 bits a channel) is about 55 MB a
  surface and its MSAA 2 image 110 MB, so about 600 MB is made and freed per game frame. Nothing is pooled.
- They are freed only as Granite's frame contexts come round. While behind, kf4-pc **owes** up to
  `FRAME_CONTEXTS_OWED_MOST = 8` contexts (pcr.cpp around 2060-2085, `frame_context_owed`), so frees lag further.
- CPU cost is small (copy_surfaces 2.6% of kf4-pc). The cost is GPU copies ("copy" 0.30 ms a picture), peak memory
  and allocation churn.

Fix direction: keep a small pool of surface-copy images (two sets: kf4-pc fills one while the pump draws on the
other), reused while the size and format are the same. Also look at whether the pump needs full copies at all
(copy only what the pictures between draw over).

## 4. A fixed ~2 ms a picture of full-screen work

`--stats` GPU kinds at every map: "2d" 1.3-1.9 ms, copy 0.3, texture copy 0.2, picture 0.1 a picture: about 2 ms of
4.4 that does not depend on the scene. GPU by shader state at 009 (`profile.py --gpu-detail`, saved at
`~/.cache/kf4sweep/prof9/samples.txt`, read with `tools/sampler/stacks.py <samples> out/linux-clang/kf4 --gpu`).
Single draws a picture that cost a lot:

- `native 000 | untextured | blend (Cs-Cd)*FIX+Cd | DATE stencil | Z >= write`: 0.32 ms (1 draw)
- `2d | surface copy modulate rgb | Z always`: 0.23 ms (1 draw)
- `2d | untextured | Z always`: 0.17 ms (1 draw)
- `2d | sampled modulate rgba | blend | DATE stencil`: 0.13 ms (1 draw)

These are the PS2's full-screen tricks (fades, copies, clears) at 4x with MSAA 2. Candidates: draw them without MSAA
where they cover the screen uniformly, merge clear+fill, skip a copy whose result is unchanged. Measure with
`--gpu-detail` before and after; pictures must stay byte for byte (`tools/tour_compare.py` against `--renderer gs`).
